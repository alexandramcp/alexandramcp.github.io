Global Health Equity & Ethics

My work in global health focuses on equity, ethics, and representation — particularly how systems, narratives, and policies shape health outcomes for vulnerable populations. This includes research on decolonising global health, ethical imagery, and the structural determinants of health.

Child Health and Vulnerable Groups

I am particularly interested in improving healthcare access and outcomes for unaccompanied asylum-seeking children (UASC) and other refugee and migrant young people. My work integrates infectious disease, mental health, and service design to promote holistic, equitable care models, including leading the CYPSAR (Children and Young People Seeking Asylum and Refuge) advocacy workstream within the International Child Health Group.

Paediatric Infectious Disease
